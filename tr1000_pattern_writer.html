    How to read it
    
      
      
    • Keys show velocity by fill height. Tags on a key: P probability, C cycle, the sub-step, or a note offset. Hatched keys sit past that track's LAST and never play.
      • 
      
    • Play is a rough browser sketch of the timing, not the TR-1000's sound. Its shuffle, track start and per-track cycle counting are estimates of the hardware.
      • 
      
    • Track length is shared by all variations, matching the machine. Whether a short track restarts when a variation changes is unconfirmed, so check that by ear on the unit.
